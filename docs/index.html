P-HR-X1

vibecoded with Claude

This is the app itself, running in your browser: the drawing and input code of the Android app, ported one to one from Kotlin to JavaScript. Same screens, menus, options, texts (English / German), visualizers and animations as in the APK. Only the music is simulated – folders, tracks and the audio signal – and About shows the data of the device you are viewing this on. Settings are kept in your browser. Download the APK
